OUR PURPOSE

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

  • To be a widely acclaimed locally and internationally college that strives to provide our graduates with an academic foundation that will enable them to gain admission to institutions of higher learning.
  • To prepare and motivate our learners for a rapidly changing world by instilling in them critical thinking, innovativeness, entrepreneuring and problem-solving skills.
  • To provide student-centered educational programs that challenge all students to their highest performance.
  • To facilitate our learners to study abroad in countries like USA, INDIA & CHINA.
  • To get our learners access to Class 4 driver's licence and other vocational courses certificates.

Our Mission

  • To develop globally-minded learners who are compassionate, curious, and ready to make an impact on the world.
  • The school cultivates critical thinkers, fosters a love of learning, and prepares students to be responsible citizens in a diverse world.
  • To create an inclusive environment that empowers students to become lifelong learners and leaders of tomorrow.
  • To develop and maintain positive relationship with the community.
  • To connect with all stakeholders to continually build a strong community.
  • To create 21st century classrooms that foster learning, provide differentiated learning opportunities, and promote the use of advanced technology to enhance student learning and produce better results.
  • To provide a safe and nurturing environment where students can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.
  • To inspire and prepare students to succeed in a rapidly changing world.

Application Procedure

  1. Complete software admission form.
  2. Submit the required documents.
  3. Attend an admission assessment.
  4. Receive admission confirmation through electronic communication.
  5. Pay the required fees.
  6. Report to the school.

Required Documents

  • Birth certificate
  • Results slip or transfer letter
  • Report book of previous school
  • Passport size photographs
  • Copy of parent or guardian ID
  • Proof of payment of admission/tuition fees
  • Typed and printed admission form
